# ApnoMart — Business Intelligence Dashboard
#Top Insight: Office Supplies delivers the highest category profit and weighted profit margin, while Electronics has the lowest weighted margin and deserves a closer review of costs and discounts.

## Project Overview

The **ApnoMart Business Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, regional trends, and business patterns. It transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights to support data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives

- Monitor key business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyze sales and profit across product categories and regions.
- Identify monthly sales trends.
- Compare profitability and profit margins across categories.
- Explore the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Generate actionable business insights and strategic recommendations.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** — Calculated measures and KPI calculations
- **CSV** — Source dataset

## Dataset

The project uses the ApnoMart sales dataset containing **1,200 records and 15 columns**, including order dates, regions, product categories, sales, discounts, profit, customer segments, and payment methods.

**Reporting period:** May 1–October 28, 2026.

## Dashboard Features

### Page 1: Executive Dashboard

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Profit Margin
- Sales by Category
- Sales Trend over Time
- Profit by Region
- Discount vs Profit analysis
- Interactive regional slicer

### Page 2: Profitability Analysis

- Profit by Category
- Profit Margin by Category
- Sales by Payment Method

The dashboard provides interactive filtering to explore business performance from different perspectives.

## Key Business Insights

1. **Furniture leads in sales:** Furniture generated approximately 3.52 million in sales, contributing around 21.63% of total sales.

2. **Office Supplies has the strongest profitability:** Office Supplies generated approximately 1.18 million in profit, with a weighted profit margin of 34.80%. Electronics recorded the lowest weighted category margin at 18.88%.

3. **Corporate customers contribute the most revenue:** The Corporate segment generated approximately 4.31 million in sales, accounting for around 26.51% of total sales.

## Strategic Recommendation

Review Electronics pricing, product costs, and discount policies to identify opportunities to improve profitability. Maintain strong relationships with Corporate customers and monitor category-level profit margins alongside sales growth to support sustainable business performance.

## Key Performance Measures

- **Total Sales:** Sum of sales revenue
- **Total Profit:** Sum of profit
- **Total Orders:** Count of orders
- **Profit Margin %:** Total Profit divided by Total Sales, multiplied by 100
